# AI Governance Portfolio Workbook

A practical demonstration of AI inventory management, risk categorisation, governance control assessment, gap analysis, and executive reporting — six linked pages, one per workbook sheet, built around six illustrative AI systems at a fictional mid-sized organisation.

## Scope

Six illustrative AI systems — a productivity copilot, a customer support chatbot, a recruitment screening tool, an invoice anomaly detector, a security log triage agent, and a meeting transcription assistant — spanning corporate services, customer service, HR, finance, and cybersecurity. Two systems (recruitment screening, security triage) carry a **High** risk tier and remain in **Pilot** status pending full assessment.

## Frameworks referenced

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage
- ISO/IEC 42001 — AI management system concepts
- EU AI Act — risk-based classification approach
- UK AI regulation principles — safety, transparency, fairness, accountability, and contestability

> **Disclaimer:** All systems, ratings, owners, dates, and findings in this workbook are synthetic portfolio examples, not verified facts about any real organisation. Replace the sample entries with verified organisational data before any operational use.
